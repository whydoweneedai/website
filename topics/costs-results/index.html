Costs & Results

Is it worth the time and money? Calculate costs, measure a pilot and decide whether to keep, fix or stop.

Count the work that remains

An automation pays off only if its benefits exceed the time and money needed to set it up, run it and correct it. Compare a pilot with your current process, including human review, exceptions and maintenance. Time saved is useful, but it is not automatically cash saved.

  • Before a trial, use the AI automation ROI guide to build a baseline, include recurring costs and make your assumptions visible.
  • After a trial, use the pilot evaluation framework to choose whether to keep it, fix a specific problem or stop.

Agree on acceptable errors and review responsibilities before you start. A fast result that needs frequent correction may be worse than a slower, reliable process. If you need ideas for a bounded test, explore the workflow guides and experiments; if the software itself is the obstacle, revisit tool selection.
