Getting Started

Where should you begin? Understand when AI helps, choose a first task and check whether your business is ready.

Start with a task, not a tool

The best first step is to choose a small, repeatable piece of work and describe what a correct result looks like. If the steps and decisions are predictable, a checklist or ordinary automation may be enough. AI becomes worth testing when the task involves varied language or inputs that simple rules cannot handle well.

  • Not sure where to begin? Use the first-task guide to find a manageable starting point.
  • Before choosing software, compare AI with rules-based automation.
  • Check the process, data and review arrangements with the readiness checklist, then see how it behaved on 12 made-up workflows.

A readiness score is a prompt for investigation, not permission to automate. Once the task is clear, move to choosing a tool and define a small test before committing.
