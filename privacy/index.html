Privacy policy

This page explains what information whydoweneedai.com collects, why it is collected, and which services may process it.

Effective November 4, 2026

Information you provide

If you complete the AI ReadyScore assessment, the site may collect information you enter such as your first and last name, company, email address, phone number, assessment answers, score, and result category. That information is used to provide or follow up on the assessment and to understand which AI topics are useful to visitors.

Contact details from the AI ReadyScore form (your first and last name, company if you give it, email address, and phone number) are used to send your results email and for follow-up. That email may mention AI consulting from Nocos Consulting, the firm behind whydoweneedai.com.

If you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you submit so we can respond.

Analytics and device information

The site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4). Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location derived from network information, browser and device characteristics, referral sources, and interactions with the site. This information helps us understand site usage and improve the experience.

You can opt out of Google Analytics in supported browsers using Google's opt-out add-on: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Advertising

This site runs Google AdSense ad code. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (also available at https://www.google.com/settings/ads). You may opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at https://www.aboutads.info/choices/ and, for users in the European Economic Area, at https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/.

Learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can read more about Google's advertising technologies at Google's advertising policies page.

Service providers

We use service providers to operate the site and its forms. Based on the current site setup, these can include Netlify for website hosting and form processing, Google Analytics for traffic measurement, Google AdSense for advertising, and Google Apps Script / Google Sheets for receiving AI ReadyScore submissions. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

How information is used

  • To provide assessment results and related follow-up.
  • To respond to messages and support requests.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website.
  • To understand which pages and tools visitors use.
  • To display and measure advertising where enabled.

Data retention and choices

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, operational needs, and any applicable legal obligations. Browser controls and privacy tools may allow you to block or delete cookies. Blocking some technologies can affect how parts of the site work.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for business and general adult audiences and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website, advertising setup, or service providers change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
