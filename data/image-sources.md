# Image sources

All 24 story images used on the homepage are real photographs hosted on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/). Each is used under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/), which allows free commercial use and does not require attribution. Attribution is listed here as good practice.

None of these photos are Unsplash+ premium images, and none are marked editorial-only. Photos with prominent brand logos, trademarks, or identifiable consumer products that could imply endorsement were skipped.

Files were downloaded from the Pexels image CDN, cropped to 16:9, and saved as 1200×675 JPEGs under 250 KB, using the article slug or news subject as the filename.
