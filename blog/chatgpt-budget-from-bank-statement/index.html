Turn last month's bank statement into a budget you'll actually keep

Holiday spending starts this month. Strip out the personal details, paste in one statement, and get a budget built on how you actually spend.

Oct 28, 2026•Updated Nov 4, 2026•7-minute read•By Ross C, editor

Researched and drafted with the site's AI writing assistant. Edited, checked, and approved by Ross C. How we work

In this article

Budgets written from memory tend to be optimistic. Your bank statement isn't. It already lists every coffee, every subscription you forgot about, and every fee. The problem is that nobody wants to spend an evening sorting 35 lines into categories. That sorting is a job you can hand to AI. With the holidays starting this month, last month's statement is the best budgeting tool you have.

The short answer: Export last month's transactions and delete everything except the date, description and amount. Sign in to a free ChatGPT account and start a Temporary Chat, which OpenAI says isn't used to train its models. Then paste the lines in with the prompt below. The prompt asks for category totals, your recurring charges, flags for fees and possible duplicates, and a budget for next month. You can also upload the file instead of pasting it (3 uploads a day on a free account). In our test with a made-up statement, ChatGPT got every total right and caught a double charge, two bank fees and a trial that turned into an annual charge. It did get one percentage wrong.

Step 1: Export one statement

In your bank's website or app, export last month's transactions for your main checking account. Choose CSV if it's offered, since it's the easiest to clean up.

Step 2: Strip out anything that identifies you

Do this before anything goes into a chat. Delete every row and column except date, description and amount. That removes your name, address and account number. If you only have a PDF, copy just the transaction lines.

Step 3: Keep the chat out of training

Free account (our pick): Use a Temporary Chat. Start a new chat, select Temporary, and turn personalization off (Unpersonalized) before you type anything. According to OpenAI's Temporary Chat FAQ, it won't appear in your history, won't create memories and isn't used to train models, though OpenAI may keep a copy for up to 30 days for safety. Don't save it afterward. For a permanent change instead, turn off Improve the model for everyone under Settings → Data controls, and turn off Memory under Settings → Personalization.

No account: OpenAI says that when you're signed out, you can open Settings and turn off Improve the model for everyone, and the choice is saved in that browser. When we tried it signed out at chatgpt.com on Nov 4 2026, we couldn't turn the setting off. The page footer said, "Chats may be reviewed and used to improve our AI models." If you can't switch it off either, assume the chat may be used for training. For a real statement, use a free account and a Temporary Chat instead.

Either way, skip the thumbs-up and thumbs-down buttons. OpenAI says that if you rate a response, the entire conversation may be used for training, even after you've opted out.

Step 4: Paste the lines and the prompt

Paste your cleaned transaction lines, then this prompt below them:

Above is my checking account statement for October. I removed my name and account numbers.

1. Put every transaction into a category (housing, groceries, eating out, transportation, utilities and phone, subscriptions, debt payments, savings, shopping, health, fees, other). Give me a table with each category's total and its share of my spending.
2. List every recurring charge and subscription with the amount and date it hit.
3. Flag anything that looks like a duplicate, a fee, or a charge I might not expect.
4. Using my actual income from this statement, build a November budget. Include a line for holiday gifts and keep savings at least where it is now. Show it as a table I can paste into a spreadsheet.

Show your math. If you can't read a line, list it separately instead of guessing.

With a free account, you can attach the CSV instead. Click +, then Add photos & files. Free accounts get 3 file uploads a day, so upload one clean file rather than several drafts.

Step 5: Check the totals yourself

Add up your statement's withdrawals and compare the sum with the chatbot's total. ChatGPT may leave savings transfers out of "spending", as it did in our test below, so add them back before you compare. If the totals still don't match, ask which lines it skipped. If a category looks off, ask: "List every transaction with the category you put it in." Recheck any percentages, too. It's the difference between a budget and a guess.

What you end up with

Test run with made-up data. The statement below is invented: every merchant, amount and date is fictional. We sent it to ChatGPT once, signed out at chatgpt.com, on Nov 4 2026 at about 11:11 a.m. ET. We used the exact prompt above and asked no follow-up questions. The page didn't show which model answered. Because the data was fake, we went ahead even though we couldn't switch off training (see Step 3). This is one run, so yours may differ.

The statement has 35 lines: two paychecks, two transfers to savings, and 31 bills and purchases. We hid four things a careful reader should catch: a café charge that shows up twice, two bank fees, and a free trial that turned into a $79 annual charge. The totals: all correct

ChatGPT reported income of $4,750.00, "actual spending" of $3,071.47, $500.00 moved to savings and $1,178.53 left over. All four match our script to the cent. Note that its "spending" leaves out the savings transfers. It said so, but it means the figure is $500 less than the sum of every withdrawal ($3,571.47).

The flags: all four caught

  • The double café charge. It listed Bean There Cafe twice on October 11 at $5.85 each and said: "These are identical transactions on the same date." It suggested checking your receipt and disputing one if you made only one purchase.
  • The trial that turned annual. It flagged the $79.00 FreshPrint charge on October 26 as an "Unexpected charge", saying "This suggests an introductory trial converted into a paid annual subscription", and put it first: "I'd investigate this one first."
  • Both bank fees. It caught the $12.00 monthly maintenance fee, which it put at $144 a year, and the $3.50 out-of-network ATM fee, both on October 27. It noted that the ATM fee "appears alongside a $60 ATM withdrawal on October 28", and added that the statement "doesn't establish whether the fee relates to that particular withdrawal."

It also listed every recurring bill with the right amount and date, and correctly called FreshPrint an annual charge rather than a monthly one.

The budget adds up to the $4,750 income exactly. It keeps savings at $500, includes the holiday-gifts line we asked for, and came with a tab-separated copy for pasting into a spreadsheet. Two assumptions are worth knowing:

  • The $30 for subscriptions leaves out FreshPrint, "pending your decision about cancellation."
  • The $0 for fees "assumes you avoid the maintenance and out-of-network ATM fees." That only works if your bank will waive the $12 fee, so ask before you budget zero.

Where it slipped

  • One wrong percentage. It wrote that housing and debt "together account for $1,889, or 61.49% of spending." The $1,889 is right, but $1,889 is 61.50% of $3,071.47. It's a small slip, but it's the kind you only catch by checking.
  • No line-by-line list. The prompt says "Show your math", but the reply gave category totals without saying which transaction went where. It stated the $3,571.47 total of withdrawals without showing the sum. The totals were right this time. If one ever looks wrong, ask for the list (see Step 5).

Evidence. Download the evidence files (zip): the full made-up statement, the exact text we sent, ChatGPT's full reply as text, our answer key, the checking script and its reports, a run record, screenshots of the conversation, and SHA-256 fingerprints.

So what?

A budget built from what you actually spent beats one built from what you hope to spend. Do it this week, before the holiday charges start piling up. Strip the file, use a Temporary Chat, and check the total.
